1. Introduction

Welcome to Navya Ayurveda. We are a wellness brand inspired by the traditions of Indian Ayurveda, and we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the website or submit your personal details to us.

This policy applies to all pages of our website, including the home page, About Us, Career, FAQ and Contact Us pages, as well as any messages you send us through the forms on those pages, by email or by phone. It does not apply to other websites or social media platforms that we may link to, which have their own privacy practices.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information that is needed to run this website and to respond to you. The information falls into the following groups.

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, city, the subject and content of your message, and, for job applications, details such as your qualifications, experience, preferred role and any resume or link you share.
  • Information collected automatically: technical details such as your browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location based on IP address, pages viewed, time spent on pages and the website that referred you.
  • Information from communication: records of the emails, calls or messages you exchange with us, so that we can follow up properly.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as bank details, passwords or government identity numbers through this website. Please do not send such information in your messages.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for clear and limited purposes, which include:

  • responding to your enquiries, questions and feedback;
  • reviewing job applications and contacting suitable candidates;
  • sharing information about Navya Ayurveda and Ayurvedic wellness when you ask for it;
  • understanding how visitors use the website so that we can improve its content, speed, design and accessibility;
  • keeping the website safe, preventing misuse and fixing technical problems;
  • meeting our legal and regulatory obligations where required.

We do not sell your personal information. We will not use your details for purposes that are very different from the ones above without informing you or asking for your consent first.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember simple preferences, to keep the website working properly and to understand overall traffic and usage patterns. Some cookies are essential for the website to function, while others help us measure performance.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies, or alert you when a cookie is being set. Please note that blocking some cookies may affect how certain parts of the website work. If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non essential cookies in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

5. Sharing of Information

We treat your information with care and share it only in limited situations:

  • Service providers: trusted partners that help us run the website, such as hosting, email, analytics or form handling services. They may use your information only to provide their services to us and must keep it protected.
  • Legal reasons: when we are required by law, court order or a lawful request from a government authority, or when needed to protect our rights, safety or property, or that of others.
  • Business changes: if Navya Ayurveda is ever merged, restructured or transferred, your information may be passed on to the new owner, who will be expected to respect this policy.
  • With your consent: in any other case where you have clearly agreed to the sharing.

6. Third Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to other websites and services, including our Instagram page, social media buttons, embedded maps and fonts or icons delivered by external providers. When you click on such a link or load such content, the third party may collect information such as your IP address and browsing activity under its own privacy policy.

We do not control these third parties and are not responsible for their content or practices. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every website or platform you visit through our links, especially before you share any personal information there.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. For example, general enquiries are kept for as long as is useful to answer you and handle any follow up, and job applications may be kept for a reasonable period so that we can consider you for current or future openings.

When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or anonymise it so that it can no longer be linked to you. If you would like us to delete your information earlier, you can write to us at any time using the contact details given below.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure and alteration. These steps include limiting access to those who need it and choosing reputable service providers.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You also play a part in keeping your information safe, for example by using secure networks and not sharing sensitive details through open channels. If we become aware of a data breach that affects you, we will act promptly and inform you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, including the laws of India such as the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and request a copy;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to erase your information when it is no longer needed;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent;
  • object to or ask us to limit certain uses of your information;
  • make a complaint about how your information has been handled.

To use any of these rights, please contact us. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has shared personal information with us, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

11. Wellness Information Notice

The content on this website is shared for general education and inspiration about Ayurveda and natural living. It is not medical advice and is not meant to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Please do not send detailed medical records, test reports or other sensitive health information through the website forms. If you need medical guidance, speak to a qualified healthcare professional.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology or legal requirements. When we make changes, we will post the updated policy on this page and revise the “Last updated” date at the top. We encourage you to review this page regularly.
